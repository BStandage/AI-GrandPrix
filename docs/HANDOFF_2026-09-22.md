## 2. What the build does (the autonomy, in order)

1. **Pad.** Waits for the pilot. Learns the accelerometer bias at rest
   (`bias=(..) n=` on every heartbeat; the PAD line every 2 s also reports the
   gate the camera sees, its range and elevation). Wait >= 10 s before arming.
2. **Takeoff.** 1250 punch open loop until airborne (~0.8 s). Then height comes
   ONLY from the gate's elevation: 0.09 m per degree, capped at 0.20 m until g0
   is crossed (0.45 m/s climb) and 0.35 m after (the stack needs 0.72 m/s).
   Damping is the estimator's `vz_inertial` (accelerometer, pad bias
   subtracted, 30 s leak). The barometer is never in the vertical channel.
3. **Release.** Up 2 s and within 0.6 m of the start, or 4 s regardless within
   1.5 m. No velocity check, no vertical dwell.
4. **Approach.** Tracks the plan at 1.5 m/s, nose on the next gate, fixes from
   every frame (position across the bearing, weakly along the range). The last
   6 m to an aligned gate are flown on the GATE'S CENTRE LINE, not the plan's
   spline (the plan jogs 1.2 m to g1's line in its last 1.5 m).
5. **COMMIT** (ring spans 85 % of the frame, 3 frames) only if: map says the
   gate is within 6 m AND at least 1.5 m ahead, heading within 35 deg of the
   crossing heading, within 1.0 m of the gate's line, elevation level within
   0.12 m and |vz_inertial| < 0.1 m/s. At 3.2 m (both edges clip) it fires
   regardless. Committed = no position fixes, height reference frozen, the
   hover throttle of the last second held (damped on vz_inertial, 200 us per
   m/s), lateral steer toward the gate line up to 8 deg, nose on the crossing
   heading.
6. **Crossing** counted 0.75 m PAST the plane, never early. Then the next gate.
7. **Missed gate** (plan past it 0.5 m for 20 ticks, no count): counted, cap
   dropped, estimator's gate count advanced, move on.
8. **Stacked gate**: the plan climbs to 4.05 m for the top opening (vision on
   the upper ring), reverses 1.69 m south of the stack, descends on the plan's
   vz when no elevation is live, commits to the low opening only once it is
   1.5 m ahead. The detector flags a stack (blob height > 1.5 x width), gives
   both ring centres, the runtime aims at the ring the next event's z selects,
   range from the width. **This last part has never run on hardware or in
   the sim** (the synthetic camera cannot model it); it was checked on one
   clean stacked frame of the organizer's video only.
9. **Finish**: no auto-disarm under vision. The pilot lands him.

Switches (env in front of the command): `AIGP_ACCEL_BIAS=0` (no pad bias),
`AIGP_COMMIT_STRAIGHT=0` (full DR lateral loop inside commit),
`AIGP_COMMIT_HOLD=0` (the OLD barometer vz hold after commit - do not).

What is now known to work on the aircraft, in one flight each: the takeoff
and release, the approach height on the elevation with hover 1205 and
aim-low, the lateral onto the gate's line, the size commit. What never
worked: the three seconds after commit. The barometer speed is +-0.4 m/s of
noise (200 us per m/s of it moved him 0.7 m), the accelerometer drifts, and
the clipped ring's elevation is garbage. The unflown candidate is the
hover-throttle hold with a light trim (`AIGP_HOLD_KD_PWM=60`, the default
`AIGP_COMMIT_HOLD=1`, `AIGP_VZ_BARO=1`), latching the 4 s mean throttle.

The next thing to build is not another hold rule: it is a vertical speed the
aircraft can trust for three seconds. Optical flow on the floor, a rangefinder,
or the ring's top edge tracked through the crossing.
