tuned against a simulator. End of day: both drones configured and reachable,
the thrust model measured from real flight data, and the plans rebuilt for the
camera we actually have.

### The single most valuable hour

Mining the recovered blackbox. The config's thrust model was seeded from the
simulator's plant:

| | was (SITL) | measured | error |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hover_pwm` | 1240 | **1291** | 51 PWM |
| thrust at 1291 PWM | 12.77 m/s^2 | **9.81 m/s^2** | **30 % over** |

The altitude loop works in acceleration and inverts that curve to get a
throttle. Flying on the old curve, the first takeoff would have been
under-thrusted by about 25 %. That was the largest single risk on the board and
it is now closed, without flying.

## Lessons learned

| # | Lesson | What it cost us |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Label the hardware.** Two identical drones, both reporting hostname `dcl-orin`, no external markings | Hours. We applied a config fix to one drone and tested it on the other |
| 2 | **Three USB ports look identical**: the FC's, the Jetson's, and the wrong laptop's | Repeatedly "plugged in" to the wrong board. The fix was reading USB vendor IDs: `0955` is NVIDIA, `0483` is the flight controller |
| 3 | **Betaflight's "Connect (Virtual)" connects to nothing** and looks exactly like success | 40 minutes. The tell is the traffic counters reading 0 % up and down |
| 4 | **The USB network gadget is unreliable**; the serial console on the same cable is not | The fix is a COM port at 115200. WiFi is better still, and untethers the bench |
| 5 | **Never tune on simulator-only data.** Our own repo has the scar: per-gate pose offsets hand-searched to 29 s on ground truth, now switched off because they did not transfer | Informed the whole design of the debrief tool |
| 6 | **There is no ground truth on the real aircraft.** Any diagnostic that needs one is a simulator toy | Rebuilt the debrief cam from scratch around two things the drone can measure about itself |
| 7 | **The accelerometer is a thrust sensor.** It measures specific force, so `abs(acc)/acc_1G * g` IS specific thrust - no attitude needed | This is what made the thrust curve recoverable from someone else's log |
| 8 | **The simulator's plant is not the aircraft.** Giving the follower the real thrust curve while flying the sim mis-commands the sim | Two config sets now, `*.toml` and `*_sim.toml` |
| 9 | **Measure at the right distance.** camcal at 2.3 m gave fy 952; at 6 m it gave 776. Close means the gate fills the frame, where lens distortion is worst | An hour of contradictory numbers |
| 10 | **Do not move the drone between calibration runs.** Two runs at a nominal 6 m disagreed 7 % because it shifted | Same hour |
| 11 | **The ladder is not monotonic.** k 0.9 failed 3 of 3 while 0.8 and 1.0 both flew clean | Fly the rungs you tested. Never interpolate a new one at the track |
| 12 | **Slower is not automatically safer.** Dead-reckoning drift grows with time, not distance, so a slow lap spends longer blind between gates | Still being tested |
| 13 | **The detector fires on non-gate red.** 287 detections in 287 frames with no gate in the room | Gave us a confident, entirely fictitious lens measurement |
| 14 | **Config rows are not portable between drones.** `aux` row 2 is free on one and carries a live mode on the other | Pasting one drone's line into the other silently deleted a flight mode |
| 15 | **Keep local copies.** The WeTransfer link expired 25 hours before we needed it | Recovered only because Cristhian still had the files |

## Where the numbers came from

| Number | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `hover_pwm` | 1291 | Four flights agreed: 1288, 1296, 1287, 1293 |
| `curve_acc` | measured | Weighted quadratic anchored at hover = 1 g, residual 0.29 m/s^2 over PWM 1150-1650 |
| `a_lat_rate_max` | 150 | Peak roll rate p99 443 deg/s = 152 m/s^3 at 45 deg tilt |
| `vz_up_max` | 1.25 | Unchanged, now validated: the human's clean lap used p95 1.27 m/s |

### Perspective on how hard we are asking the aircraft to fly

| | human's clean race lap | our slowest rung (k 0.25) | our fastest (k 1.0) |
|---|---|---|---|
| tilt | 8 deg | 28 deg | 50 deg |
| lateral-accel slew | 27 m/s^3 | 68 | 150 |

A human flew a clean two-lap run at 8 degrees of tilt. Our most conservative
plan asks for more than three times the slew. Worth sitting with.
